Privacy Policy

Renamr File Renaming Application
Last Updated: May 25, 2025
Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how Renamr handles your information and protects your privacy.
The Short Version: Renamr does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or information about you or your files. Everything happens locally on your device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Renamr is designed with privacy as a fundamental principle. We do not collect any information from you, including but not limited to:

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiers
  • File Information: We do not collect, store, or transmit information about your files, file names, file contents, or file metadata
  • Usage Data: We do not collect information about how you use the app, which features you use, or how often you use the app
  • Device Information: We do not collect information about your device, operating system, or hardware
  • Location Data: We do not collect or access your location information
  • Analytics Data: We do not use analytics services or collect any usage statistics
  • Crash Reports: We do not automatically collect crash reports or error logs

How Renamr Works

Renamr is a local file processing application that:

  • Processes files entirely on your local device
  • Does not connect to the internet for any functionality
  • Does not transmit any data outside of your device
  • Only accesses files that you explicitly select or provide to the application
  • Modifies file names locally according to your specified preferences

File Access

Renamr only accesses files when you:

  • Drag and drop a folder into the application
  • Use the file browser to select a source directory
  • Choose a destination directory for renamed files

The app only reads file names and metadata necessary for the renaming process. It does not read file contents or store any information about your files.

Data Storage

Renamr may store the following information locally on your device for app functionality:

  • User Preferences: Your chosen settings such as base names, numbering preferences, and file extension filters
  • Application State: Window positions and interface preferences

This information is stored locally using standard macOS preferences systems and is never transmitted outside of your device.

Third-Party Services

Renamr does not integrate with or use any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics services (such as Google Analytics)
  • Crash reporting services
  • Cloud storage services
  • Social media platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Payment processors (beyond Apple's App Store)

Children's Privacy

Renamr does not collect any information from users of any age. The app is safe for users under 13 years of age as no personal information is collected or transmitted.

Changes to Files

Renamr modifies file names according to your specifications. Depending on your settings:

  • Rename in Place: Original files are renamed directly
  • Copy to New Location: Renamed copies are created while originals remain unchanged

These operations happen entirely on your local device and do not involve any external services or data transmission.

Security

Since Renamr does not collect or transmit any data, there are no data security risks associated with external data handling. Your files remain entirely under your control on your local device.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to:

  • Access or download
  • Correct or update
  • Delete or erase
  • Port to another service

You maintain complete control over your files and any locally stored preferences can be reset by reinstalling the application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Since Renamr does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. We recommend checking this policy periodically for updates.

Compliance

This privacy policy is designed to comply with:

  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • Connecticut state privacy laws
  • Other applicable privacy laws

Since no personal data is collected, most privacy law requirements do not apply to Renamr's operation. This policy is governed by the laws of Connecticut, USA.
